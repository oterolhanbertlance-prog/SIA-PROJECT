Boba House POS v16
- Cashier no longer uses customer cart/checkout.
- Customer orders enter a branch-specific Cashier Order Queue.
- Cashier receives in-app new-order alerts on all staff pages.
- Cashier confirms customer payment and completes/cancels orders.
- Customer pickup and delivery orders require a branch.
- Sales Receipts show orders processed by the cashier.
- Auth redirects normalized to appBaseUrl to prevent Apache 404 paths.
