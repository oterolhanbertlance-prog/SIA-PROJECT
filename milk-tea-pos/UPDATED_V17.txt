Boba House POS v17
- Customer cart now persists by customer account in customer_cart_items.
- Logout preserves the cart; login restores it.
- Saved carts are revalidated against current product name, price, stock and ingredient availability.
- Remove, quantity update and Clear Cart immediately update the saved database cart.
- Successful order submission clears the saved cart; failed checkout keeps it.
- Staff accounts do not inherit or persist customer carts.
