POS v31 - Stock/availability consistency fix

- Product stock and ingredient stock are now treated separately and consistently.
- A product with stock > 0 is no longer marked unavailable just because a stale
  ingredient is_available flag remained off.
- Required ingredients only block a product when their actual stock_quantity is 0.
- 'Ingredient Missing' was changed to the more accurate 'Ingredient Out of Stock'.
- Restocking/editing an ingredient automatically restores its availability flag.
- Customer menu, add-to-cart, saved cart, topping checks and checkout use the same stock rule.
- Existing v30 cashier order fix and product customization behavior are preserved.
